## 03. CO-Inertia Analysis (COIA):

Codes produced to perform COIA to join the molecular descriptors (cleaned and filtered with gLASSO) and the behavioural ($B$-spline coefficients, aggregation on the median of the IGT metric) databases to study potential relationships between the molecular structure of chemical contaminants and the diversity of avoidance behaviours in three invertebrates used in aquatic ecotoxicology.
